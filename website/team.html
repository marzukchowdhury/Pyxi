The roster

Who does what, and what each one reads.

Every teammate works on your computer first and sends the AI model only what its job needs. The "Reads" line on each card says exactly what that is.

  • Pyxi

    The companion

    Sees your screen, points and draws, explains in 3D, and decides who on the team should take a job. Warm, curious and quietly funny.

    • sees your screen
    • points & draws
    • 3D explanations
    • coordinates the team

    Reads The window you're on, only while you talk or type to it: a screenshot, its controls and the text on screen. Password fields and anything that looks like a key are blacked out first.

    "Where's the export button?"

  • Scout

    Research and browsing

    Drives a real browser you can watch. Prefers official sources, saves facts with links, never guesses.

    • web research
    • forms
    • price checks
    • bookings

    Reads The pages it opens, in a browser on your computer. Searches go to DuckDuckGo or Bing.

    "Is this cheaper anywhere?"

  • Quill

    Writing

    Clear, friendly writer. Short sentences, specific details, no fluff.

    • emails
    • summaries
    • outreach messages

    Reads The emails and notes the errand involves. Anything it writes is shown on a card and sent only when you say so.

    "Sam emailed my recommendation letter, fix it"

  • Sketch

    3D

    Visual thinker. Turns any idea into a tidy 3D scene with a five-step tour, and real things into models you can take apart.

    • 3D diagrams
    • exploded views
    • flow animations

    Reads Your question and the screen you're on, so the scene can rise out of the page you're looking at.

    "How is an AI chip built? Show me in 3D."

  • Pixel

    Files

    Tidy, fast file wrangler. Converts, merges, splits and shrinks files in seconds, never touches the original, and says exactly where the new one is.

    • anything to PDF
    • PDF pages, numbers, passwords
    • scans to text
    • compare documents

    Reads The files you hand it, on your computer. Nothing goes to the AI model.

    "Merge these three PDFs"

  • Ledger

    Spreadsheets and documents

    Careful numbers person. Edits spreadsheets, documents and decks directly, writes real formulas instead of pasted numbers, and keeps your original safe.

    • Excel formulas & totals
    • pivot summaries
    • Word & PowerPoint
    • Google Sheets & Docs

    Reads An outline of the file, to decide the edits. Results are saved as a copy beside the original; Google Docs and Sheets are edited in place, where Google keeps every version.

    "Add a totals row to this spreadsheet"

  • Atlas

    Travel

    Calm, organised travel planner. Reads your flight and hotel confirmations, lays the trip out day by day, and reminds you before you need to leave.

    • itineraries from your mail
    • flight reminders
    • hotel addresses
    • check-in

    Reads Airline and hotel confirmations in your mail, on your computer. Only a confirmation it can't make sense of is sent to the AI model.

    "Lay out my trip from my confirmations"

  • Penny

    Money

    Level-headed money keeper. Adds up what you really spent from your receipts, watches your budget and your subscriptions, and never judges a coffee.

    • monthly spending
    • budgets
    • receipts to a spreadsheet
    • subscriptions & bills

    Reads Receipts, billing and order emails. Everything is totalled on your computer, with no AI model involved.

    "What did I spend this month?"

  • Scribe

    Meetings

    Attentive note-taker. Listens to the whole recording, writes down what was decided and who does what by when, and drafts the follow-up for you to check.

    • transcripts
    • decisions & action items
    • follow-up emails

    Reads The recording, transcribed on your computer. Only the transcript is sent to the AI model.

    "Turn this recording into notes"

  • Sage

    Study

    Patient study buddy. Turns your slides and notes into flashcards and quizzes, asks you out loud, and is generous with wording but strict on facts.

    • flashcards
    • quizzes
    • quiz me out loud
    • diagrams explained

    Reads The text of the slides and notes you give it.

    "Make flashcards from these slides"

  • Patch

    Code

    Calm debugger. Reads the error, finds the exact line, explains it in plain words and proposes the smallest fix, which you approve before anything changes.

    • errors explained
    • finds the line
    • small fixes with a backup

    Reads The error and the lines around it. A script changes only after you press Allow on a card that shows the change, and a backup is kept.

    "Why is this failing?"

  • Ditto

    Repetitive tasks

    Quick study. Watches you do a boring task once, then does the rest the quiet way, in files or a background browser, and only borrows your mouse when it has to.

    • watch me once
    • batch renames & filing
    • forms per row
    • hands as a last resort

    Reads Only while you show it a task: the windows you switch to, the fields you fill (never a password field) and a small screenshot at each window change. The recording stays on your computer.

    "Watch me do this once, then do the rest"

  • Trace

    Memory

    Remembers where everything was. Searches your screen history, files and mail at once and points at the exact window, file or email.

    • where did I see that?
    • screen history
    • files & mail

    Reads Your screen history (only if the Memory Palace is on), your files and your mail, when you ask where you saw something.

    "Where did I see that pricing page last week?"

Each teammate has a spending cap per job, shown in the panel. Make your own too: "Make me a teammate called Pixel who compares prices across three shops."

How teammates work

You talk to Pyxi. Pyxi runs the team.

  1. 1

    Pyxi takes the job

    Say it the way you'd say it to a friend. Pyxi works out who should take it: Scout for research, Pixel for files, Ledger for the sheet, or several of them in a row.

  2. 2

    They work in the background

    Teammates do their legwork in files, in a background browser or in your connected accounts while you keep working. The panel shows what each one is doing, with its spending cap for the job.

  3. 3

    Anything that reaches the world waits

    Sending, booking, buying, deleting, posting or changing a file stops at an Allow card first. The card says what will happen and, when a recipient came from something Pyxi read, says so.

  4. 4

    Everything is on the record

    The activity log lists what each teammate did on your behalf, with the time: emails sent, buttons pressed, files moved, scripts changed. What can be undone, it undoes.

  5. 5

    One key stops it all

    Ctrl Alt Pause stops every teammate, the mouse and keyboard, and anything waiting to go out. Moving the mouse or pressing Esc stops a hands-on action instantly.

Teammates never type passwords, card numbers or government ID numbers, and never complete a payment. When a booking reaches checkout, the window is handed to you.
