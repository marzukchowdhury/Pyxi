What Pyxi does

It shows you.
It doesn't just tell you.

With most AI you paste a screenshot in and read paragraphs out. Pyxi already sees what you see, and it answers on top of it: a ring on the right button, a note beside the paragraph, a model you can watch being built.

It looks when you ask

When you talk or type to Pyxi, it takes one look at your screen: the picture, and the names and positions of the controls on it. Nothing in the background unless you turn that on.

It answers on your screen

Rings, arrows, handwritten notes, tags on pictures and 3D scenes, each drawn the moment Pyxi says it. You hear the answer and see it in the same place.

It does it when you say so

Pyxi can move your mouse and type after you press Allow. Move the mouse or press Esc and you're back in control on the spot.

Explains

Notes beside the thing you're reading.

Ask "explain this page" or "what's the story in this chart?" and Pyxi writes a notebook card right next to it. Each point writes itself in ink as Pyxi says it, and a numbered outline frames the exact thing it means. Ask about a diagram or a photo and it pins short tags onto the actual parts of the picture.

  • Explain this meme
  • What's the story in this chart? Draw it.
  • Explain this diagram

Guides

Points at the button. Then waits for you.

Ask how to do something in any app and Pyxi marks the one control to click, with an arrow from your cursor. It waits for your real click, looks again, and gives you the next step until you're done. Name the app and it looks at that window, even on your other monitor.

  • How do I add an environment variable in Vercel?
  • Walk me through turning on notifications
  • What does this setting do?

Builds in 3D

Ask how something works. Watch it get built.

"Hey Pyxi, how is an AI chip built? Show me in 3D." Pyxi assembles a real-looking model from parts: the substrate, the silicon interposer, the compute die and the stacked memory chips beside it. Then it takes it apart layer by layer while it explains, like a short video. Say "show the inside" or "make it spin" and it edits the same model. Ideas get the same treatment: HTTPS, OAuth or a neural network become a 3D scene with a guided tour.

  • How is an AI chip built? Show me in 3D.
  • How are RAM chips stacked?
  • How does OAuth work?

Takes the wheel

Your mouse and keyboard, only when you say so.

Delegates

A small team that works while you watch.

For bigger jobs Pyxi hands off to teammates with their own names, voices and memory. Scout researches in a real browser, Quill writes, Sketch makes 3D. They pass work along: Scout finds the pricing, then Quill writes the summary. Afterwards, any browser job replays as a Ghost Walkthrough that shows you how to do it yourself.

  • Scout, find the pricing for linear.app, then Quill, write me a two-line summary
  • Make me a teammate called Pixel who compares prices across three shops

Remembers, if you want

A Memory Palace for your screen.

Turn it on and Pyxi keeps a quiet history of what was where. "Take me back to the pricing page this morning" rebuilds it as an explorable hologram with a time scrubber, and "how did I get here?" lays out the chain of events in 3D. It's off until you turn it on, records no keystrokes, stays on your computer and clears itself after 72 hours.

  • Take me back to what I was doing 20 minutes ago
  • How did I get here?

Privacy

It can see your screen. So it's careful.

Privacy is the core of a product like this, not a footnote. Here's the short version. The full policy says plainly what Pyxi looks at, what leaves your computer and how to delete it.

Read the privacy policy
  • Looks only when you ask. Pyxi captures your screen when you talk or type to it. The Memory Palace is off by default.
  • Secrets are blacked out. Password fields, password managers, banking apps and anything that looks like a key or token are removed before anything is sent.
  • Not sold, not used for training. What you ask goes to an AI model to work out the reply. We don't sell it, and we don't use it to train models.
  • History stays on your PC. Conversations clear after 30 days, Memory Palace history after 72 hours.
  • You hold the controls. Mouse and keyboard only after Allow. Ctrl Alt P pauses all screen access.

FAQ

Good questions.

How do I join the beta?

Download and install Pyxi, open Settings, choose Join the Pyxi beta and type the invite code you were given. That's it. The install guide walks through every step.

Do I need an API key or a paid AI account?

No. In the beta, Pyxi's AI runs through the Pyxi beta server, so an invite code is all you need. Each beta account has a free daily allowance that resets at midnight UTC.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The beta installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher and shows "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway. The install guide shows exactly where to click.

Does Pyxi watch my screen all the time?

No. It looks only when you talk or type to it. If you turn on the Memory Palace, it keeps window titles, focus changes and where controls were, on your computer, for 72 hours. It never records keystrokes, and Ctrl Alt P pauses everything.

Can it really use my mouse and keyboard?

Yes, after you press Allow (once, or Always allow). It labels what it's doing beside the cursor and stops the moment you move the mouse or press Esc. Sending, paying, deleting, posting, signing in or out and closing apps always ask again, and it never types into password fields.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11. Pyxi reads the screen with Windows UI Automation, so Windows comes first. A Mac version is next on the roadmap, and you can join the Mac waitlist.

Does anything run offline?

Speech recognition (Whisper) and the default voice (Kokoro) run on your computer. Understanding your screen uses an AI model online. People who build Pyxi from source can also run it fully locally with Ollama.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps, find Pyxi and choose Uninstall. To remove your history too, delete the %APPDATA%\Pyxi folder.
