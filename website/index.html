      Asked first

      Does Pyxi watch my screen all the time?

      No. It looks only when you talk or type to it. If you turn on the Memory Palace, it keeps window titles, focus changes and where controls were, on your computer, for 72 hours. It never records keystrokes, and Ctrl Alt P pauses everything.

      What leaves my computer?

      Your request, the masked screenshot and on-screen text, recent conversation, and the parts of files or emails a job needs go to an AI model to work out the reply, through Pyxi's server and OpenRouter. We don't sell it and don't use it to train models. Pyxi's server doesn't store what you ask, what's on your screen or your voice. The privacy policy has the full table.

      Can it really use my mouse and keyboard?

      Yes, after you press Allow (once, or Always allow). It labels what it's doing beside the cursor and stops the moment you move the mouse or press Esc. Sending, paying, deleting, posting, signing in or out and closing apps always ask again, and it never types into password fields, never types card or government ID numbers, and never completes a payment.

      What does Pyxi cost?

      The first 3 days are free. After that Pyxi is a subscription with three plans: Starter at $9 a month, Pro at $19 and Max at $39, or $90, $180 and $372 a year. Prices are in US dollars, and there's no free plan. Each plan comes with a monthly usage allowance, and a bigger plan has a bigger one. The pricing page compares them.

      How does the free trial work?

      The trial is 3 days of Pro, and no card is asked for. It starts the moment you sign in with Google inside Pyxi, once for each Google account, and it comes with a small allowance of its own. When it ends, Pyxi asks you to choose a plan to keep going. There's no sign-in on this website: everything happens in the app.
