What Pyxi does

  1. 01 / 05

    Most AI lives in a chat box.

    You paste a screenshot in and read paragraphs out.

  2. 02 / 05

    Pyxi lives on your screen.

    It already sees what you see, and it answers on top of it.

  3. 03 / 05

    It looks when you ask.

    One look at the window you're on: the picture, and the names and positions of the controls on it. Nothing in the background unless you turn that on.

  4. 04 / 05

    It answers right where you're looking.

    Rings, arrows, handwritten notes and 3D scenes, each drawn the moment Pyxi says it. You hear the answer and see it in the same place.

  5. 05 / 05

    And it does the clicking when you say so.

    Pyxi can move your mouse and type after you press Allow. Move the mouse or press Esc and you're back in control on the spot.

Explained in 3D

Ask how it works.
Pyxi builds it on your screen.

Not a picture and not a video. Pyxi puts a real-looking model together from parts, in real 3D, on top of whatever you have open, and talks you through it step by step. Turn it around, look inside, or ask for a change and the same model changes.

How does a car engine work?

Pyxi's walkthrough spoken out loud in the app

    • Built from parts, as you watchPyxi describes the thing as parts with real materials: metal, glass, rubber, paint. They show up on your screen as each one is ready.
    • Told like a short videoEvery step has its own camera angle, the parts it is about, and a sentence or two that Pyxi says out loud. Parts that are in the way fade so you can see.
    • Yours to changeSay "show the inside", "remove the casing" or "make that part move". Pyxi edits the model you already have instead of starting again.

    Both models are Pyxi's own, drawn live in your browser rather than played from a video. In the app they float over your real windows and Pyxi tells the story out loud. Here the steps are written beside the model.

    How it works

    Three layers.
    One glance.

    1. Your screen, as it isWhen you talk to Pyxi, it takes one look at the window you're on. Nothing in the background.
    2. What Pyxi readsEvery control, its name and exactly where it sits, from Windows UI Automation. Password fields are blacked out before anything is sent.
    3. What Pyxi drawsRings, threads, notes and 3D models on a see-through layer above your apps. Your clicks go straight through it.

    Move your mouse over the window: wherever Pyxi hovers, the screen turns to glass. Hover a step to hold it.Pyxi's lens roams the window on its own. Tap a step to hold it.
