What Pyxi does

It shows you.
It doesn't just tell you.

With most AI you paste a screenshot in and read paragraphs out. Pyxi already sees what you see, and it answers on top of it: a ring on the right button, a note beside the paragraph, a model you can watch being built.

It looks when you ask

When you talk or type to Pyxi, it takes one look at your screen: the picture, and the names and positions of the controls on it. Nothing in the background unless you turn that on.

It answers on your screen

Rings, arrows, handwritten notes, tags on pictures and 3D scenes, each drawn the moment Pyxi says it. You hear the answer and see it in the same place.

It does it when you say so

Pyxi can move your mouse and type after you press Allow. Move the mouse or press Esc and you're back in control on the spot.

How it works

Three layers.
One glance.

  1. Your screen, as it isWhen you talk to Pyxi, it takes one look at the window you're on. Nothing in the background.
  2. What Pyxi readsEvery control, its name and exactly where it sits, from Windows UI Automation. Password fields are blacked out before anything is sent.
  3. What Pyxi drawsRings, threads, notes and 3D models on a see-through layer above your apps. Your clicks go straight through it.

Move your mouse over the window: wherever Pyxi hovers, the screen turns to glass. Hover a step to hold it.Pyxi's lens roams the window on its own. Tap a step to hold it.

Watch it work

It works the way you would.
Only with your OK.

Ask in your own words. Pyxi explains what's on your screen right where it is, writes the email, books the meeting, and sends Scout off to research in the background while you keep working. Anything that reaches another person, or costs money, waits until you press Allow.
