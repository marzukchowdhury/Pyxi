If something's off

Quick fixes.

Nothing happens when I hold Ctrl Alt

Hold-to-talk uses the left Alt key, so AltGr typing never triggers it. You can also press Ctrl Alt Space to start and stop. If the microphone is blocked, the notch says so: allow Pyxi in Windows Settings, Privacy and security, Microphone.

I don't see "Join the Pyxi beta"

Make sure you're on the Settings tab of the full panel (the expand button in the notch). If you've already joined, the card says "You're in the beta" and shows today's allowance instead.

How do I stop Pyxi seeing my screen for a while?

Press Ctrl Alt P to pause all screen access, with a visible indicator. Press it again to resume.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings, Apps, Installed apps, Pyxi, Uninstall. To remove your history too, delete the %APPDATA%\Pyxi folder. See the privacy policy for what's stored there.
