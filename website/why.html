Three kinds of help

A chat box. A browser agent.
Or someone who's already looking.

A chat box

You describe the problem, or paste a screenshot in, and read paragraphs out. Then you go back to your screen and find the button yourself.

A browser agent

Lives inside a browser tab and drives web pages for you. Anything outside the browser, a document, a settings window, a game, is out of reach.

Pyxi

A firefly beside your cursor that already sees the window you're on. It points, writes on the page, builds it in 3D, or does the clicking after you press Allow, in any app.

Who it's for

Anyone who'd rather be shown than told.

Students

Ask about the slide you're on and get the explanation on it, with a 3D model if that helps. Sage turns your slides and notes into flashcards and quizzes.

People who email and book a lot

Quill drafts the reply beside the message. Atlas pulls your trips together. Bookings fill in and stop at the final step, which is yours. Everything that sends waits for Allow.

People who want it explained where they are

A post, a chart, a bill, an error message. Pyxi explains it on the spot with a cue card and a thread of light to the part it means.

Bengali speakers

Pyxi speaks English and Bengali. Hold Ctrl Alt and ask in whichever comes first.
