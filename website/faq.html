    Pyxi

    Getting started

    Installing and joining.

    Which computers does it run on?

    Windows 10 and Windows 11, and Macs on macOS 12 or later. The Mac version is a newer beta: download it here, and the install guide shows how to open it the first time.

    Windows 10 and Windows 11. Pyxi reads the screen with Windows UI Automation, so Windows comes first. A Mac version is next on the roadmap, and you can join the Mac waitlist.

    Windows 10 and Windows 11. Pyxi reads the screen with Windows UI Automation, so Windows comes first. A Mac version is next on the roadmap.

    How do I install it?

    Click Download for Windows and save {{downloadFile}}. If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, open the download's menu and choose Keep. Run it: Pyxi installs in one click for just your account, with no admin rights needed. A little firefly appears beside your cursor and a slim glass notch sits on the top edge of your screen. The install guide shows every step.

    Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

    The beta installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher and shows "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, check that the app is {{downloadFile}}, then Run anyway. The install guide shows exactly where to click.

    How do I join the beta?

    Install Pyxi, press Ctrl Alt W or click the Pyxi icon in the system tray to open the notch, click the expand button for the full panel and choose Settings. At the top, under Join the Pyxi beta, type the invite code you were given and click Join. That's it. The install guide walks through every step.

    Where do I get an invite code?

    Invite codes come from the Pyxi team during the beta. No code yet? Email {{contactEmail}}.The beta is invite-only for now.

    Does the beta cost anything?

    No. Pyxi is free during the beta, and you don't need an API key or a payment card. Each beta account has a daily allowance of AI use that resets at midnight UTC, and the whole beta has a monthly limit. The terms of use spell this out.

    Do I need an API key or a paid AI account?

    No. In the beta, Pyxi's AI runs through the Pyxi beta server, so an invite code is all you need. If you'd rather use your own key, the beta card in Settings has Use my own key instead.

    Why does Pyxi say it's in demo mode?

    Demo mode is what Pyxi runs when it has no beta token and no key: a rule-based stand-in with no AI model, so you can see the firefly move and try a few built-in tricks. Join the beta from Settings with your invite code and the real brain takes over.

    Using Pyxi

    Talking to it, and letting it work.

    How do I talk to Pyxi?

    Hold Ctrl Alt, ask about anything on your screen, and let go to send. Tap Ctrl twice to type instead. Ctrl Alt W opens the notch, and the expand button opens the full panel with Chat, Team, Memory, 3D, Voice and Settings.

    Which languages does it speak?

    English and Bengali. Your speech is turned into text on your computer. The one exception is Bengali, which is sent to a cloud model to be understood, as the privacy policy explains.

    What can it actually do on my screen?

    It answers out loud, points at the right thing with a ring and a thread of light, writes notes on the page, builds explanations in 3D, and does the clicking for you when you let it. Anything that reaches another person or costs money waits for your OK. The What it does page shows each one.

    What are the teammates?

    Pyxi leads and hands jobs to teammates that work in the background while you keep going: Scout researches, Ledger edits spreadsheets and documents, Pixel converts and merges files, Atlas builds trips from your confirmations, Penny totals your spending, Sage makes flashcards, Scribe turns a meeting recording into notes, Patch fixes a script, Forms fills a form from a document, Trace finds where you saw something, and Ditto learns a task by watching you once. Meet them on The team.

    Can it really use my mouse and keyboard?

    Yes, after you press Allow (once, or Always allow). It labels what it's doing beside the cursor and stops the moment you move the mouse or press Esc. Sending, paying, deleting, posting, signing in or out and closing apps always ask again, and it never types into password fields, never types card or government ID numbers, and never completes a payment.

    What are Allow cards?

    Before Pyxi sends, books, buys, deletes or posts anything, a card shows exactly what will go out, and nothing happens until you press Allow. An "always" grant expires after 30 days, and Settings lists every grant so you can revoke it. The card also says when a recipient came from something Pyxi read rather than from you.

    How do I stop it?

    Say "Stop", move the mouse, or press Esc: it stops at once. Ctrl Alt Pause (also in the tray and in Settings) stops everything together: every teammate, the mouse and keyboard, and anything waiting to go out. Ctrl Alt P pauses all screen access.

    Can it use my email and calendar?

    If you connect them. Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Discord and LinkedIn are optional, and until you connect one Pyxi can't see it. Google and LinkedIn sign you in on their own websites, so Pyxi never sees your password. Sending an email, posting, messaging or inviting someone always waits for an approval card first.

    Does anything run offline?

    Speech recognition (Whisper) and the default voice (Kokoro) run on your computer. Understanding your screen uses an AI model online. People who build Pyxi from source can also run it fully locally with Ollama.

    Privacy

    What it sees, and what it sends.

    Does Pyxi watch my screen all the time?

    No. It looks only when you talk or type to it. If you turn on the Memory Palace, it keeps window titles, focus changes and where controls were, on your computer, for 72 hours. It never records keystrokes, and Ctrl Alt P pauses everything.

    What about my passwords?

    Password fields, password managers, banking apps and credential windows are blacked out before anything is sent, and anything that looks like an API key or token is removed from the text and the screenshot. Pyxi never types into password fields, and it refuses to remember a password, card number, government ID or key even if you say one.

    What leaves my computer?

    Your request, the masked screenshot and on-screen text, recent conversation, and the parts of files or emails a job needs go to an AI model to work out the reply, through OpenRouter (during the beta, through the Pyxi beta server). We don't sell it and don't use it to train models. The beta server doesn't store what you ask, what's on your screen or your voice. The privacy policy has the full table.

    How long is my history kept?

    Conversations, with the masked screenshot each answer used, stay on your computer and are deleted after 30 days. Memory Palace history, if it's on, is deleted after 72 hours. Everything lives in %APPDATA%\Pyxi\.pyxi.

    How do I delete everything?

    Wipe everything in the Memory tab forgets every memory, the Memory Palace and the conversations with their screenshots. It asks first. Disconnect an account in Settings to remove its tokens. Uninstalling Pyxi and deleting %APPDATA%\Pyxi removes everything.

    What does Pyxi do with my Google data?

    Only what you ask it to do. Pyxi's use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: it isn't sold, used for advertising or used to train models, and it isn't stored on our servers.

    Account and beta

    Allowances, updates and leaving.

    What happens when I use up the allowance?

    Pyxi tells you it has used today's beta allowance and stops answering until it resets at midnight UTC. The whole beta has a monthly limit too. Opening apps, music and the other things Pyxi does without the AI model keep working.

    Can I leave the beta?

    Yes. The beta card in Settings has Use my own key instead, which leaves the beta on this PC. Your beta token is stored on your computer, encrypted with Windows' own data protection. To have your beta token and spend totals deleted from the beta server, email {{contactEmail}}.The privacy policy explains how to have your beta token and spend totals deleted.

    How do updates work?

    Pyxi checks for a new version when it starts and every four hours, downloads it from GitHub in the background, and installs it when you restart or quit. The tray menu has Check for Updates if you don't want to wait.

    Is Pyxi open source?

    Pyxi's source code is published under the MIT License, and it includes other open-source parts under their own licenses. The terms of use cover the installer, the beta server and the beta itself.

    How do I uninstall it?

    Open Windows Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps, find Pyxi and choose Uninstall. To remove your history too, delete the %APPDATA%\Pyxi folder.
