What counts. A question is one thing you ask: an answer, an explanation of what is on your screen, a pointer or a diagram. A 3D model is one model Pyxi builds for you. An agent task is one job Pyxi does for you, such as a task on your desktop or an errand on the web.

Need more? A top-up is $5 for 25 more 3D models or 100 more agent tasks. There is no free plan: every plan starts with the 3-day trial of Pro, and the trial asks for no card.

Download for Windows All questions
1What happens when I run out?

Pyxi tells you. You can top up, or wait for the next month, when your plan fills up again.

2Can I change my plan?

Yes, at any time.

3Does my data leave my PC?

Some of it has to, so Pyxi can answer. The privacy page says exactly what is sent, what is kept and how to delete it.

4Is there a free version?

There is a 3-day free trial of Pro, with no card needed. There is no free plan after it.

5How do I start, and how do I pay?

Download Pyxi and sign in with Google. Your 3-day trial starts then. To keep going, open Settings in Pyxi and choose a plan: you pay on a secure checkout page in your browser, and Pyxi unlocks a few seconds later. There is no sign-in on this website.
