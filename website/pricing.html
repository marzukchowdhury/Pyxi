How much you get on Pro

Monthly allowanceA little over twice Starter

How the allowance works. Each plan comes with a monthly usage allowance, and the plans differ only in how big it is. Whatever Pyxi's AI does for you uses some of it: a quick answer uses a little, and a 3D model or a long job by a teammate uses more. Settings in Pyxi shows how much you have used, and the allowance fills up again at the start of each month.

The trial. There is no free plan: every plan starts with the 3-day trial of Pro, and the trial asks for no card. The trial comes with a small allowance of its own. When it ends, you choose a plan in Pyxi's Settings to keep going.

Fair questions.
Straight answers.

Nothing here is hidden in small print. If your question is not on the list, the FAQ has more.

Download for Windows All questions
1What happens when I run out?

Pyxi tells you, with the date your allowance fills up again. You can wait for that date, or move up a plan from Settings in Pyxi.

2Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, at any time, from Settings in Pyxi. Change plan and Manage subscription open your subscription page in your browser, where you can move to another plan, update your card, see your receipts or cancel.

3Does my data leave my PC?

Some of it has to, so Pyxi can answer. The privacy page says exactly what is sent, what is kept and how to delete it.

4Is there a free version?

There is a 3-day free trial of Pro, with no card needed. There is no free plan after it.

5How do I start, and how do I pay?

Download Pyxi and sign in with Google. Your 3-day trial starts then. To keep going, open Settings in Pyxi and choose a plan: you pay in US dollars on a secure checkout page in your browser, and Pyxi unlocks a few seconds later. There is no sign-in on this website.
