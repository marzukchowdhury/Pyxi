# Pyxi try-out: everyday basic tasks

Say each line to Pyxi, one at a time, and wait for it to finish before the next one. You don't
need to take notes: I'm watching every turn live (what Pyxi heard, what it did, how long it took)
and fixing what goes wrong. If something looks wrong to you, just say so in our chat, for example
"7 was weird".

**Time targets:**
- **Opens and media:** about 1 second.
- **Anything else:** starts within 5 seconds.
- **Background work (Ledger, Scout):** done within about 30 seconds.

**Before you start:**
- Have Firefox open. Some music or a YouTube video should be playing for part E.
- Have one empty Notepad window open for part F.

Nothing here sends, buys or deletes anything.

## A. Opening things (instant)
1. "Open a blank spreadsheet." A new Google Sheet opens.
2. "Open Gmail." Gmail opens in a new tab.
3. "Go to YouTube."
4. "Open notepad."
5. "Open the calculator."
6. "Open Bluetooth settings."
7. "Open Google Docs on a new tab."
8. "Create a new Google doc."

## B. Google Sheets (do it, don't coach)
Start on the blank spreadsheet from step 1.
9. "Make a pivot table with some sample sales data." A sheet with a sample table and a pivot appears on screen. If the open sheet is in your other Google account, Pyxi makes a new sheet in its own account and opens that.
10. "Add a chart of sales by region." Ledger adds a chart.
11. "Make the header row bold."
12. "Add a total row at the bottom."
13. "How do I make a pivot table myself?" This one SHOULD coach you, one click at a time. Press Esc to stop it.

## C. Writing
14. "Open a new Google Doc and write a short welcome note for my team."
15. Click into Notepad, then say: "Write a three-line packing list for a weekend trip here." It types it into Notepad.
16. "Copy the first line of that to my clipboard."

## D. Looking things up (answers, no clicking)
17. "What's the weather in Vancouver tomorrow?"
18. "Find a coffee place near me that's open now." Google Maps opens and Pyxi tags its picks.
19. "How many ounces are in a liter?"
20. "What's 18 percent tip on 64 dollars?"

## E. Music and sound
21. "Pause the music." This should be instant and silent.
22. "Play it again."
23. "Next song."
24. "Turn the volume down a bit."
25. "Play Blinding Lights by The Weeknd."

## F. Windows and tabs
26. "Put Firefox and Notepad side by side."
27. "Open a new tab with Google Maps."
28. "Zoom in on this page a bit." Uses the mouse and keyboard; asks to Allow first.
29. "Put all my Firefox tabs into one window." Only if you have two Firefox windows. Uses the mouse. Keep your hands off the mouse while it works.

## G. Understanding the screen
30. With any article or post on screen: "What's this about, in two sentences?"
31. "What does this button do?" Hover over any button first.

## H. Follow-ups and changing your mind
32. "Open a blank spreadsheet." Then, right away: "Actually, make it a doc."
33. "Stop." It should stop whatever it's doing at once.
34. "Thanks, that's all."

## What I watch for
- **Coaching:** it tells you to click when you asked it to do something.
- **Slowness:** more than 5 seconds to start, or more than 30 seconds for a background job.
- **Repeating:** it says the same thing twice, reads out a tool name, or talks too long.
- **Getting stuck:** it does the same thing over and over, or ends up on the wrong window, tab or account.
- **False results:** it says it did something it didn't.
